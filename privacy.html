Privacy Policy

Last updated 13 September 2026.

What we collect

When you book a class or propose one to teach, the form sends us your name, contact details and what you're booking or proposing. When you rent the space or message us, whatever you share by LINE, Messenger, phone or email is what we have.

How it's used

Only to arrange your class, session or rental, and to reach you about it. We don't sell or share your details with third parties, and we don't use it for marketing unless you ask us to keep you posted.

Where it goes

Booking and proposal forms are delivered by email through FormSubmit, a third-party form relay. It handles the message in transit; we don't use it to build a mailing list.

Cookies and tracking

This site doesn't use cookies, analytics or ad tracking. Your light/dark theme choice is remembered in your browser's local storage, on your device only; it's never sent to us.

Your choices

Ask us any time to tell you what we hold about you, or to delete it, at thelittlesomething.studio@gmail.com.
