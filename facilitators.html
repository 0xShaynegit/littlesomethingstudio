For teachers and makers

Create your own class

Don't see your practice here? Bring it. Weekly classes, one-off workshops or full-day sessions, in Thai, English or both.

Women's circles, life drawing, cacao ceremony, journaling nights, ecstatic dance, paint jams, breathwork, ukulele or singing circles: if it fits on a wooden floor, bring it.

  • We help fill your floor. Hosting here isn't just renting a room, it's joining a supportive platform. We actively showcase your upcoming classes to our growing community of local students and practitioners.
  • Designed for versatility. From mirrored wall alignments for dance and capoeira to open floor layouts for breathwork circles, our 3rd-floor space adapts effortlessly to your vision.
  • Simple, transparent booking. Host a recurring weekly practice, launch a weekend immersion, or test a brand-new concept with flexible hourly rates and simple setup.
