Terms of Service

Last updated 13 September 2026.

Bookings

Booking a class through this site or on LINE holds you a spot for that session. Sessions run at the times and prices shown at the time of booking; occasional changes to schedule or price will be communicated where possible.

Payment

Pay in cash on arrival or by PromptPay QR sent after booking. No card details are collected online.

Cancellations

Please message us on LINE as early as you can if you can't make a booked session, so we can offer the spot to someone else. Repeated late cancellations or no-shows may affect future bookings.

Facilitators

Classes are run by independent facilitators who rent time in the studio. They are not employees of The Little Something Studio. Each facilitator sets their own class content, style and any additional guidance given during a session.

Studio conduct

We ask everyone in the space to be respectful of facilitators, other participants, the building and its neighbours. The studio may decline a booking or ask someone to leave for behaviour that puts people or property at risk.

Renting the space

Rental bookings are agreed individually by message or email, covering dates, times and rate. See Rent the space for details.

Governing law

These terms are governed by the laws of Thailand.
