Our story

Made for movement, connection and creativity

The Little Something Studio is a bright, comfortable room above Mae Hia market, with a wooden floor and the mountains through the glass.

We made it for the teachers and makers of Chiang Mai: a warm place where yoga, dance, capoeira, painting and whatever comes next can find a home.
